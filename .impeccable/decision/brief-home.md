## Scope

Home page of belfamuhammad.com (one long page: intro, work, process, pricing, contact). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: small-business owners and personal brands comparing freelancers. Action: the contact form. Proof: three labelled concept projects plus Belfa's real marketing background. Mobile-first.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a marketer's work desk. Projects lie on it as printed cards you pick up and flip, and the back of every card is the marketing brief behind the design. It refuses the category default: a centred hero over a grid of equal screenshot cards.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal desk ground (#1C1B19) with faint grain; off-white card stock (#F2F0EB) in ink (#141414); signal orange (#FF5B1F) as the only accent, used for tape strips, flip marks and the primary action. One type family, Archivo: condensed heavy caps for display, normal width for text. Cards carry real paper depth (offset, soft blur), slight rotation, tape. No glass, no gradients, no icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor understands Belfa is a web designer who thinks like a marketer, believes it because each card's back shows the goal, audience and desired action behind the work, and sends an enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: name top-left, orange "Start a project" top-right; display headline "PREMIUM WEBSITES, DESIGNED BY A MARKETER." in condensed caps across the full width; below it a loose, rotated stack of project cards with the top card flippable, and the line "Based in Indonesia, working with clients worldwide." Desktop: headline in the left five columns, cards scattered across the right seven, draggable.

FORM: User-pinned from Belfa's references (a polaroid wall of work; project covers as physical objects), superseding the roll; position: pinned. seed: ff10e73146e0054b

Signature interaction: the card flip (front: project; back: marketing brief), by tap, click or Enter; cards can be dragged on desktop. Motion grammar: cards settle with an exponential ease-out; flip 500ms; reduced motion swaps faces instantly with no rotation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
