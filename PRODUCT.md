# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of hospitality businesses worldwide: villas, boutique hotels, restaurants, cafés, and wellness/spa venues. They are evaluating a remote freelancer, usually from a link shared in an email, a directory, or social media, and deciding whether this person can bring them more direct bookings and covers. Many are non-native English speakers, and many check the site on a phone.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio site of **Belfa Muhammad**, a brand & website partner for hospitality businesses. It exists to win remote clients. Success means a qualified owner sends an enquiry through the contact form.

## Positioning

Marketing, brand identity, and website in one person. Belfa has roughly 6–7 years in digital marketing, marketing, branding, and brand identity, including online marketing and reservations for hospitality businesses (social media, booking platforms such as Booking.com and Airbnb, guest communication). The websites are built to fill rooms and tables, not just to look good. Based in Indonesia, working with clients worldwide.

## Operating Context

- Primary action: the contact form, with messages delivered to Belfa's email.
- Structure: one main page (intro, work, process, pricing, contact) plus one case-study page per demo project.
- Content language: English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS, deployed to Vercel via GitHub.
- Fonts from Google Fonts, icons from Lucide, motion subtle and respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- SEO basics on every page (metadata, Open Graph image, sitemap, structured data). Ready for Google Analytics through an environment variable; no real ID in the code.
- Pricing shown as "Websites from $900". Brand identity pricing: undecided.
- Open: contact email address, social/profile links, domain name.

## Brand Commitments

- Display name: **Belfa Muhammad**. Full name (Belfa Muhammad Ravi Firmansyah) may appear on the About section.
- Positioning line: "Based in Indonesia, working with clients worldwide."
- Experience is stated honestly and in general terms, exactly as above. No invented employer names, job titles, or achievement numbers.
- All copy passes the no-ai-slop check before it ships.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: Belfa's 6–7 years of marketing, branding, and hospitality reservations experience (described in general terms only).
- Demo work: three concept projects (01-villa, 02-kafe, 03-brand-wellness) in different countries, still to be built. Each must be labelled "concept project".
- Absent and must not be fabricated: client testimonials, client logos, booking or revenue figures, employer names, awards, press.
- Portrait photo: not yet available. Belfa will shoot a natural, casual phone photo later; reserve a slot until then.

## Product Principles

1. Bookings over decoration: every section should move an owner closer to enquiring.
2. Speak as a marketer who builds, not as a coder who decorates: talk about guests, direct bookings, and brand, in the owner's language.
3. Honesty is the trust signal: concept work is labelled, experience is described plainly, nothing is inflated.
4. Show the craft: the site itself is the first proof that Belfa can make a hospitality brand feel worth visiting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, mobile-first layout, plain English for non-native readers, reduced-motion support.
