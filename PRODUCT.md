## Users

Small-business owners and personal brands worldwide who need a website that looks premium and gets visitors to act: coaches and personal trainers, clinics, local service businesses such as contractors, villas and restaurants, and similar. Many find freelancers through Upwork or a shared link, compare several profiles quickly, and often check the site on a phone. Many are non-native English speakers.

The personal portfolio site of **Belfa Muhammad**, a web designer who understands brand and marketing. It exists to win remote clients. Success means a qualified prospect sends an enquiry through the contact form.

## Product Principles

1. Action over decoration: every section should move a prospect closer to enquiring.
2. A marketer who designs: talk about customers, trust, and results in the owner's language, not about code.
3. Honesty is the trust signal: concept work is labelled, experience is described plainly, nothing is inflated.
4. The site is the first proof: it must itself look premium and make the visitor act.
